Big Alien Venture 👽 — marketplace-style mobile website.

The design is inspired by the mobile shopping layout shown by the customer: prominent search, horizontal categories, free-shipping/safety strips, dense product grid, cart, profile, orders and bottom navigation.

Customer flow:
- First screen is login/create account.
- Products are hidden until login.
- Customer can search, browse categories, buy, checkout and track shipments.

Owner:
- Owner can add/edit/delete products and upload product pictures.
- Uploaded pictures are stored in browser storage for this prototype and appear in the marketplace.
- Owner can update shipment status; customers see the status, tracking number, estimated delivery and shipment history.

For a real public Jumia-style service, use a secure backend/database and server-side owner permissions.
